# Finetuning tools and libraries

Software for adapting pretrained models to your own data: from the parameter-efficient layers (LoRA) provided by PEFT, to full training frameworks such as DeepSpeed, to opinionated fine-tuning CLIs such as axolotl and unsloth.

- [Hugging Face PEFT](peft.html) — parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA/QLoRA).
- [TRL](trl.html) — supervised and preference fine-tuning (SFT, DPO, RLHF).
- [axolotl](axolotl.html) — a friendly YAML-driven fine-tuning tool.
- [unsloth](unsloth.html) — fast low-memory fine-tuning with big speed-ups.
- [DeepSpeed](deepspeed.html) — distributed training and optimization.
