# Model families and paradigms

Modern AI is a zoo of model architectures and training paradigms. The most important distinction is between **encoder-only**, **decoder-only** and **encoder-decoder** transformers, and between **generative**, **diffusion** and **multimodal** models.

Read about [architecture families](architecture.html), the [training paradigms](paradigms.html) and how [large and multimodal models](llm-vlm.html) fit together. For a quick reference of which models are open-weights versus closed, see the [model comparison table](model-comparison.html).
